Let's begin with Russia/Ukraine News. Ukrainian strikes on August 1st hit three adjacent oil refineries in Bashkortostan. Specifically, Bashneft-UNPZ, Novoil, and Ufaneftekhim, which together remove 470,000 barrels per day of capacity from a complex that normally processes 23.5 million tons annually. This is not the first time this refinery has been hit, but they need to be hit from time to time to keep it closed.
Cumulative Ukrainian drone attacks have now reduced Russian refinery throughput by roughly 45 percent, according to industry estimates. That's getting very close to a point of no return where the Russians cannot fund their war machine, and oil continues to degrade on its own as pumps degrade when they aren't in use because they have nowhere to store the fuel and nowhere to refine it.
